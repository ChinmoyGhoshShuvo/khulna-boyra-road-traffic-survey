**Title:** Road Cross-Section and Traffic Survey, Khulna

**Summary:** Our 5-member studio team surveyed a 3.9 km urban corridor in Khulna. We drew road cross-sections, ran classified traffic counts at three intersections over six time slots, and carried out speed, headway and moving-observer surveys. The Baikali highway intersection carried the most traffic (up to 4,060 PCU/h), and the corridor flow measured 1,889 PCU/h.
